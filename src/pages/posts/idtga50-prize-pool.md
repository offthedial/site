50 seasons! Can you belive it? Even though we have just returning from our hiatus, it's time to make this event bigger at better than ever! One of these is with the **$500 Prize Pool**. But who get's what? 

This page also server as the Terms and Conditions of the Prize Pool.

## Prize Pool
- The total Prize Pool caps at $500 USD

## Method of Disbrution
- The prize will be distributed in the form of __gift cards__ on the **Off the Dial Shop** at [shop.otd.ink](https://shop.otd.ink).

## Disbrution Split
- Master Division 1st place: $200 ($50 per player)
- Master Division 2nd place: $120 ($30 per player)
- Power Division 1st place: $88 ($22 per player)
- Wisdom Division 1st place: $60 ($15 per player)
- Courage Division 1st place: $32 ($8 per player)

## Eligibility
To be eligible for the Prize Pool you must meet all of the following conditions:
- You must be eligible for the tournament as stated at [otd.ink/idtga/rules](https://otd.ink/idtga/rules).
-  You must not violate any rules on our rules page at [otd.ink/idtga/rules](https://otd.ink/idtga/rules).
- You must follow all of our Terms and Conditions at [otd.ink/legal](https://otd.ink/legal).
- You must have registed at [otd.ink/signup](https://otd.ink/signup) before the registration closure time.
- You must achive an eligible placement, listed above in the Disbrution Split.
- If you are below the age of 18 you must have parental consent to play in the tournament.

You are not eligible if you meet ANY of the following:
- You break/don't follow any of the conditions listed above
- You are a substitute for a dropped out player.
- You dropped out of the tournament at any stage of the tournament.
> In other words, to be eligble, you need to have been on the same team from when the teams were announced by the tournament organisers and stay with that team for the entire duration of the tournament.

## Laws and Legislations

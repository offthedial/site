It's season 50!! The big five-o! We're running a few things to celebrate this season of IDTGA including...
- Double Signal Strength!
  - Earn 2x as much signal strength as a normal tournament!
- $500 prize pool!
  - All divisions will be eligible for the prize pool! See the latest blog post for the full details on the prize pool.
  - Key thing to note is that the prize pool will be distrbuted as **gift cards on the Off the Dial Shop**.
- Now on Sendou!
  - All of our backend has been synced up with Sendou now, so let's play!

**Patch Notes:**

- If you have results listed on Sendou, you are no longer required to put those results on your IDTGA signup form.
- Introduced team communication rule: all players must attempt (or show an attempt) at communicating with their team once within 24 hours of teams releasing.
- Backend migration to Sendou, which includes;
  - Players will be automatically assigned their team roles on the Off the Dial Discord based on their Sendou team.
  - When removing a dropout from the tournament, the bot will automatically remove them from the Sendou tournament.
  - The Off the Dial site will now import tournament data such as dates from the Sendou tournament.
  - Updated rules and FAQ to reflect the change from start.gg to Sendou.
- Signal Strenth Changes:
  - Now recieve results from Sendou.ink.
  - Changed the calculation formula for signal strength distrbution. You now recieve 100 points for playing, up to 100 points for placement in the Preliminaries, and up to 100 points based on placement in the Top Cut.
  - This means the highest possible amount of signal strength you can earn from winning IDTGA has increased from 200 to 300.
  - These changes are independant from the 2x multiplyer for IDTGA 50 (so the total points for winning IDTGA 50 is 600 points).
